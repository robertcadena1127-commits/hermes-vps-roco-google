Hermes VPS ROCO

Privacy and data use

Hermes VPS ROCO is a personal connector operated by the owner of rocoengraving@gmail.com. It is intended for the owner's private Hermes Agent.

Google information and permissions

With the owner's consent, the connector can read, send, and manage Gmail messages; read and manage Calendar events; and read, create, edit, or delete Drive files, Google Docs, and Google Sheets. These permissions support tasks requested by the owner.

How information is used and shared

Google information is used to complete the owner's requested tasks. Information needed for a task may be included in requests to the configured ChatGPT/Codex model provider. Requested results may appear in Hermes Desktop or the owner's approved Telegram chat. The application runs on infrastructure hosted by Hostinger. This connector does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

Google OAuth credentials are stored on the private VPS with access limited by filesystem permissions. Hermes can retain conversations, files, task results, logs, and memory on that VPS. The owner manages retained copies through the private instance. Revoking Google access stops future authorized API access but does not automatically delete previously retained information.

Control and removal

The owner can revoke the connector through Google Account connections, remove its saved credentials from Hermes, and delete retained data from the private VPS. Questions or removal requests can be directed to rocoengraving@gmail.com.

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
