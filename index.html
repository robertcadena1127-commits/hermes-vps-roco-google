Personal Google Workspace connector

Hermes VPS ROCO

Hermes VPS ROCO connects a privately operated Hermes Agent to its owner's Google account. The owner uses Hermes Desktop or an approved Telegram chat to request help with Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Google Docs, and Google Sheets.

The connector supports reading and managing email, calendar events, and files when requested by the owner. It runs on the owner's Hostinger VPS and uses the owner's selected ChatGPT/Codex model connection.

This page describes the connector. It does not provide access to the private Hermes instance, Google account, files, or credentials. There is no public registration.
